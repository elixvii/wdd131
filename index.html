Sevilla, Spain

Sevilla is a city in southern Spain, known for its rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant culture. It is the capital of the Andalusia region and is famous for its flamenco dancing, tapas bars, and beautiful landmarks such as the Alcazar Palace and the Seville Cathedral. The city has a warm Mediterranean climate, making it a popular destination for tourists seeking sun, culture, and history.
